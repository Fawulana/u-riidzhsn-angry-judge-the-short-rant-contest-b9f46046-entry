# Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046`.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes one short rant to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The self-checkout called me a thief. I scanned one bag of rice and it screamed "UNEXPECTED ITEM IN THE BAGGING AREA" like I had smuggled a grand piano. Then it froze, summoned a human, and made me stand there while the whole queue watched. I came for milk. I left with a criminal record and trust issues.

The rant: a machine that accuses you of theft over a bag of rice, escalating to public humiliation — a grievance every shopper has lived. Deterministic and offline: running it twice writes byte-identical output.
